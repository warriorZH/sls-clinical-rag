# Data licence

Our annotations, required points, labels, selector scores, selections, generated answers and judgements are released under **CC BY 4.0**.

`data/external/` contains material derived from TCM-BEST4SDT (Li et al.), which is licensed under CC BY 4.0. Attribution: Li K, et al., TCM-BEST4SDT, Figshare, DOI 10.6084/m9.figshare.30615956.

The texts of the source case records, textbooks and classical texts are **not** included, and remain subject to the terms of their original sources (a de-identified TCM case-record collection and a corpus of TCM textbooks and classical texts, assembled in an earlier stage of the project; their individual sources and licences will be documented in a later release).
